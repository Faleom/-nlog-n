# F.014 — Branch 2: Milestones & Guided Prompts

**Tier 1** · Owner: **P3** · Reviewer: P2 · Status: Implemented, self-verified — needs fresh-context review

## What
Milestone information as **narrative, not a checklist**, one open question, then
**three guided prompts** if the answer is yes.

## Depends on / Blocks
Depends on F.001, F.002. Blocks F.015.

## Done when
- Milestone info reads as prose matched to the child's age in months. **No
  checkboxes, no yes/no items, nothing that could be counted or scored** — a
  checklist that resolves to anything is a screening tool (§9.2).
- One open question: *is there anything here that's been on your mind?*
- **"No" ends the flow.** No score, no result, nothing recorded as a finding,
  no "are you sure", no re-ask next visit (§9.3).
- "Yes" → three short fields, identical every time, no branching:
  1. *What did you notice?*
  2. *How old were they when you first noticed?*
  3. *What does it look like when it happens?*
- Guided prompts, **not free text** — typing a paragraph on a phone at 11pm is
  the main abandonment risk, and structured input reduces hallucination risk in
  F.015 (§9.3).
- Tone carries no risk framing: no *warning signs*, *red flags*, *delayed*, no
  comparison to a norm the child could fail.
- Nothing on these screens is written to the log.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Any checkbox or item that could be summed or scored? (§9.2)
- [ ] Does "no" record literally nothing?
- [ ] Do the three prompts ever change or branch based on answers? They must not
      — branching is what makes something an instrument.
- [ ] Could a parent read this and come away with an implied result? (§9.2)
- [ ] Is prompt 2 phrased as a memory question, not a milestone-timing question?
- [ ] Is the Companion absent from this branch? A cheerful toy voice next to a
      worry is tonally wrong (§10).
