# F.017 — Companion Mechanic in Game 1

**Tier 2** · Owner: **P2** · Reviewer: P4 · Status: Not started

## What
Wires the Companion through Game 1 as guide, target, helper and bookend. This is
the differentiator becoming visible (§6.3).

## Depends on / Blocks
Depends on F.004, F.008, F.016. Blocks nothing.

## Done when
- **Guide**: all instructional audio framed as coming from them — *"{companion}
  wants to find something {fav_colour}!"* — never a disembodied app voice.
- **Companion hunt**: *"Where's {companion}?"* The child hunts their actual toy.
  Highest-motivation target available — **reserve it for the session's final
  trial** so it stays special (§8.1).
- **Helper who needs help**: *"{companion} can't reach the {object}. Can you get
  it for {companion_they}?"* Prosocial framing.
- **Reward**: their photo appears on success, framed in `{fav_colour}`.
- **Bookend**: greets at start, waves goodbye with the off-screen handoff.
- Target selection weighted toward `{fav_colour}`, the Companion, and objects
  near `{fav_place}` (§8.1).
- **The profile-swap demo moment**: change the Companion in settings and the
  whole app visibly changes. Four seconds, proves the architecture (§18).

## Review checklist
- [ ] Does the profile swap actually change the whole app, same code?
- [ ] Is the Companion ever sad, disappointed or hurt by a wrong answer?
      Exactly two states: waiting and delighted (§6.3).
- [ ] Does the Companion ever give an instruction the caregiver can't see on
      screen? It must not (§6.3).
- [ ] Is the Companion hunt reserved for the last trial?
- [ ] With no Companion set, does the neutral guide still work?

## Not in this file
No illustrated or licensed character — the character is a photo the parent took,
and that's the point. Companion never appears in Branch 2 (§10).
